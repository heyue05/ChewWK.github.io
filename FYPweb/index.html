PERSONAL FINANCE MANAGEMENT

Manage your money. Understand your spending.

Flowly is an offline personal finance management mobile application designed to help users organize transactions, manage multiple accounts, scan receipts, and understand their financial behaviour.

THE PROBLEM

Managing personal finances is becoming more difficult.

01

Scattered Financial Information

Financial information is distributed across different banking applications, e-wallets, and payment platforms.

02

Manual Transaction Recording

Recording transaction details manually can become time-consuming when the number of transactions increases.

03

Limited Spending Visibility

Users may find it difficult to identify spending patterns and understand where their money is going.

04

Lack of Financial Insights

Without appropriate analysis and reminders, users may not notice unusual spending or declining account balances.

02
PROJECT OBJECTIVES

One application. Three core objectives.

01

Centralized Financial Management

Provide a unified interface for managing multiple financial accounts, transactions, balances, and spending information.

02

Automated Transaction Recording

Use ML Kit OCR technology to extract relevant information from receipts and reduce manual transaction entry.

03

Spending Analysis & Reminders

Analyse transaction data and provide rule-based insights and reminders to improve spending awareness.

03
